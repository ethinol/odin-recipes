Description

This is one of those family friendly recipes that is always in our rotation. They kids love, and I love how easy it is to prepare. I’ll often double or triple the recipe when I’m having people over, or to have leftovers for adding to a taco salad lunch bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 8 corn or flower tortillas
  • Lettuce finley chopped
  • Shredded mexican cheese blend or cheddar cheese
  • Tomatoes chopped
  • Red onions chopped

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in skillet over medium high heat. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes. Drain any fat.
