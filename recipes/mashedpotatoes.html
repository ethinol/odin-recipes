Descripion

These are the BEST mashed potatoes, incredibly buttery and creamy, easy to make, and the perfect additon to any meal.

Mashed potatoes (and stuffing) are the best part of any Thanksgiving or holiday meal and the perfect side with almost anything! They go especially well with sauces, gravy or things like Salisbury steak, beef tips, or Swiss steak.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Yukon Gold or Russet potatoes, peeled and cut into even chunks
  • 4-6 tbsp unsalted or salted butter
  • 1/2 to 3/4 cup whole milk, half-and-half, or cream
  • salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Place potatoes in a large pot and cover with cold water by about 1 inch. Stir in salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 15-20 minutes until a knife slides in easily.
  2. Drain the potatoes well in a colander. Return them to the warm, empy pot over low heat for 1-2 minutes to evaporate excess moisture.
  3. Mash the potatoes using a potato masher or ricer. Avoid food procesors or overmixing, which release excess starch and makes them gummy.
  4. Fold in butter until melted, then slowly stir in warm milk or cream until smooth and creamy.
  5. Tase and adjust with salt and black pepper before serving
